1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to RupayVibe Web (https://rupayvibe-web.vercel.app), owned and operated by JP Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our web portal and participate in daily tasks, quizzes, and market research surveys.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Authentication, we collect your name, email address, profile photo URL, and Firebase User ID (UID) to synchronize your web activity with your RupayVibe mobile app wallet.
  • Technical & Device Identifiers: IP address, browser type, device operating system, screen resolution, advertising identifiers, and country/region metadata used strictly for fraud prevention, duplicate-account detection, and survey matching.
  • Activity & Reward Logs: Records of completed daily tasks, spin wheel results, quiz answers, and survey postbacks.

3. Third-Party Survey & Offerwall Partners

To provide paid market research surveys and offers, we integrate third-party offerwall providers including TheoremReach, BitLabs, and TimeWall. When you open a survey wall, your pseudonymous User ID (UID), IP address, and basic demographic/device signals are shared with the survey provider to match relevant surveys and credit your rewards via encrypted Server-to-Server (S2S) postbacks. Please review the respective privacy policies of these partners before participating in their surveys.

4. Advertising & Cookies

We display third-party advertisements (such as Adsterra) to keep our platform free for users. Advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, or local storage to serve relevant ads and measure ad impressions. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. GDPR Privacy Rights (European Economic Area & UK Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your personal data is your explicit consent (provided upon signing in and participating in tasks/surveys) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and maintaining platform security. Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Right of Access: Request copies of your personal data stored by us.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to or request limits on how we process your data, and withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), grants you specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know & Access: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, or disclose to third-party survey partners.
  • Right to Delete: Request the deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell your personal information for monetary compensation. Where sharing device identifiers or User IDs with third-party survey/ad networks is considered "sharing" under California law, you may opt out at any time by discontinuing survey use or emailing us to request data removal.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA privacy rights.

7. Data Security & Retention

All user wallet balances and authentication records are stored securely on Google Firebase infrastructure with strict access controls. We retain account data only as long as your account remains active or as required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

RupayVibe Web is strictly intended for users aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.
